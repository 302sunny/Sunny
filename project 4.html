About Coaching

Adarsh Coaching Centre is pride of our village.

Its journey started from a very small room.

Great history was created with very limited resources.

Teachers hard dedication is the real strength here.

The disciplined environment makes it completely different today.

Students learn every topic in very simple language.

Basic concepts of every student are made strong.

Special attention is given to weak students always.

This coaching is giving toppers since three years.

It achieved continuous victory in Bihar Board exams.

Everyone is highly surprised by this amazing result.

Big expensive city institutes are left far behind.

Poor and talented students got real support here.

High quality education is provided at low fees.

Success came from continuous day and night hard-work.

Special test is conducted here on every Sunday.

Special crash course runs right before the examination.

Excellent notes of teachers are very useful always.

Now this unknown village got its own identity.

This coaching centre is famous in whole Bihar.

Junior students also dream big for their future.

Hunger for success is seen here every year.

This institute is a holy temple of education.

It raised the pride of village very high.

Best wishes for the glorious journey ahead always.
